# Promotion between stages is by configured, enforced Gates

A Strategy moves backtest to paper only when the Backtest Gate passes (out-of-sample Sharpe above 1.0 after full costs, max drawdown under 15%, at least 150 trades, profit factor above 1.3). Paper to live needs the Paper Gate (90 trading days, paper Expectancy within 30% of backtest, slippage within the model, zero Limit breaches). Live with Approvals to live without needs the Autonomy Gate (90 further days, same checks). The AI layer leaves Shadow Mode only on the AI Promotion Gate (at least 200 shadow decisions, vetoed trades show significantly lower Expectancy than kept trades, stated confidence is calibrated). The thresholds are configuration, the enforcement is code, and the Operator cannot promote by hand. The gates exist because the system has no demonstrated edge; they are how we find out whether it has one.

## Amendment (2026-10-25, Phase 2b): the Backtest Gate compares with the Benchmark after tax, and the Paper Gate checks fidelity

The absolute Backtest Gate asks for a Sharpe above 1.0 and a drawdown under 15 percent. No long-only result in the literature clears that after costs (`docs/research.md`), and a fully invested portfolio, the index itself included, would fail it whatever its merit. The Operator's real alternative to running Tradeline is an index fund, so from the `lowvol` family on the Backtest Gate asks whether the Strategy beats that fund, after costs and tax. `pullback_v1`'s Gate is recorded in its frozen Protocol and stands as run.

- **The Benchmark** is the direct plan, growth option, of the Motilal Oswal Nifty 500 Index Fund, valued at its AMFI NAV, which already carries its expenses and tracking error. Its history starts 2019-09-11, before the Stitched Curve. The build asserts from AMFI's scheme master that the scheme name contains both "Direct" and "Growth", and the scheme code is recorded in the Protocol. A change of fund happens only on an event: the fund's merger, closure, or change of index, recorded with its date. The Benchmark is bought on an account's first day and pays the stamp duty, STT and exit load on fund units from the dated schedule.
- **Both sides are taxed, to Liquidation Value.** The Strategy pays tax as realised: short-term gains (held twelve months or less) at 20.8 percent and long-term at 13 percent, the 4 percent cess included and surcharge left out (the report says this is optimistic for high incomes), dividends at a configured rate defaulting to 31.2 percent; short-term losses set off against either kind of gain and long-term losses against long-term only, carried forward eight years; each Indian financial year's net tax debited from cash on the first trading day after March 31, and a final partial year settled at liquidation. The Rs 1.25 lakh long-term exemption is left out on both sides, and losses unused at liquidation are dropped and their size reported. The Benchmark pays tax once, on sale at the end, by the same holding rule, so a period of about twelve months may tax it as short-term; the report says which rate it paid. Rates are a dated schedule like costs, and, as with costs, the schedule as of the Protocol's freeze is applied to every trade and recorded in the Protocol.
- **Gated thresholds, on both the Stitched Curve and the Holdout:** annualised excess of Liquidation Value over the Benchmark's above zero after all costs and tax; drawdown of Liquidation Value no worse than the Benchmark's plus 2 points; and, on the Stitched Curve, at least 8 Rebalances. Either curve failing fails. The information ratio is reported prominently, since excess above zero over a few years can be luck at low volatility's tracking error; so are Sharpe, profit factor, Expectancy, results before tax, and the comparison with a fund for the same factor (the ICICI Prudential Nifty 100 Low Vol 30 ETF at NAV), which is reported and never gated, since that index's history before launch is backfilled.
- **The Paper Gate checks fidelity, not performance.** Two semiannual Rebalances cannot confirm a factor premium; the Fresh Period carries that evidence (ADR 0007). Paper starts with its own Initial Buy once Phase 3 is done, runs through at least two Rebalances, and must match a backtest replayed over exactly its days at its own commit: holdings to the share, each Fill within the Slippage model of the end-of-day open the replay uses, costs to the rupee, dividends, Demerger Credits and the tax ledger equal, and zero Limit breaches. A paper Run refuses to start when the Strategy version hash differs from the frozen one, or at a commit without a passing Drift Replay (ADR 0007). Expectancy within 30 percent of the backtest remains the Paper Gate of trade-by-trade families.

Alternatives rejected: keeping the absolute Gate (certain failure regardless of merit); requiring both the absolute Gate and the Benchmark (includes the same impossible test); gating before tax and reporting after (could pass a Strategy that loses to the fund once tax is paid, by a drag the size of the edge); modelling the Operator's own slab and exemption (personal, and gains on listed equity are taxed at flat rates anyway); a Paper Gate on performance against the backtest (two Rebalances are noise).
