# Scope is long-only NSE cash equities; no F&O, no intraday, no other markets in year one

Retail delivery accounts cannot hold short positions overnight, so a swing system on cash equities is long-only. Futures and options would allow shorts and index hedges but bring margin, expiry, lot sizes, and a different risk model. Intraday has the worst cost profile and the thinnest retail edge. All three are excluded until the Live Gate has passed for the cash strategy. Any of them is a new Strategy with its own Gates, not an extension of this one.
