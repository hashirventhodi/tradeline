Kite's Adjustment, replayed stage by stage

PROTOTYPE · #62  The question: does replaying Kite's adjustment one stage at a time reproduce Kite's daily closes exactly from NSE's raw closes? Each stage is what Kite did to the older history at one Ex-Date: divide every close it already held by a divisor, or subtract an amount, then round to a tick. Older closes pass through every later stage, earliest first. Nothing here is a tolerance: a close is reproduced or it is not.

These are the real closes from the ten-year #59 run. Switch stages on and off, change a divisor, a tick or how ties round, and watch which closes the chain lands on.
